## 1. Headline findings

1. **Courts and platforms draw the line at specific expression and element copying, not at voxels.**
   - In China, Mini World escaped copyright liability for its overall look. It was still liable for **unfair competition**, because it copied 230 specific element designs ([court report](https://www.sdcourt.gov.cn/lyynfy/390976/390978/9118308/index.html)).
   - Milk Block won its appeal because similar rules alone did not prove similar expression ([Guangzhou IP Court](https://www.gipc.gov.cn/front/showArticle?id=2636)).
   - In the UK, *Nova v Mazooma* holds that general game ideas are "too general to amount to a substantial part" ([judgment](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewca/civ/2007/219/data.pdf)).
   - In the US, *Tetris v Xio* found infringement where a clone copied the "overall look and feel" ([Loeb summary](https://www.loeb.com/en/insights/publications/2012/06/tetris-holding-llc-v-xio-interactive-inc); [opinion](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-njd-3_09-cv-06115/pdf/USCOURTS-njd-3_09-cv-06115-0.pdf)).
   - **Lawyer to confirm** how this applies to DeltaOps.
2. **The most likely practical risk is an automated takedown, not a lawsuit.** Microsoft's brand-protection vendor has twice in 2026 pulled voxel games off major stores (Allumeria on Steam, Luanti on Google Play). Both notices were withdrawn within days ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/i-genuinely-do-not-know-what-to-do-says-developer-of-minecraft-like-allumeria-after-microsoft-issues-a-dmca-takedown-forcing-it-off-steam/); [Luanti](https://blog.luanti.org/2026/09/08/dmca-rescinded/)). Luanti's 2023 removal lasted 46 days ([Luanti](https://blog.luanti.org/2026/08/27/luanti-dmca-tracer-ai/)). DeltaOps is browser-based and not on app stores. Its public screenshots, any store listings and video uploads are where the exposure lies.
3. **Staffed, timed, ticketed group "missions" draw large family audiences.** Villager Rescue sold 260,000 tickets in its first five months in London ([Licensing.biz](https://licensing.biz/minecraft-experience-villager-rescue-sees-phenomenal-demand/)) and passed 310,000 visitors in total there. It runs in groups of no more than 25 every 15 minutes ([Singapore FAQ](https://www.minecraftexperience.com/singapore/faq/)), and a wildlife reserve (Mandai, Singapore) hosted it from April to September 2026 ([Mandai](https://www.mandai.com/en/about-mandai/media-centre/Minecraft-Experience-Makes-Its-Asia-Debut-In-Singapore-At-The-Mandai-Wildlife-Reserve-This-April.html)).
4. **Minecraft Education's AI catalogue keeps growing.** It includes The First Night, Reed Smart: AI Detective, CyberSafe AI, Fantastic Fairgrounds, AI for Good and Generation AI ([Code.org](https://code.org/cs/minecraft)), plus a Vatican heritage world. I found **no published outcome evaluation** of any of them (see agent run sources in 3.4).
   - DeltaOps' visible, scripted "% sure" remains a differentiator. The First Night teaches the Agent with `training_data` and never shows confidence (round-2 deck, R2 source; the [blog](https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/blog/hour-of-ai-the-first-night) describes errors as the Agent "forgetting", with no confidence display).
5. **Microsoft funds the evidence that children want adults involved in learning AI.** In that survey, 84% of 9–12-year-olds who use AI want at least some help from adults ([4-H](https://4-h.org/about/blog/young-people-want-guidance-from-adults-on-use-of-ai-tools-says-first-of-its-kind-survey-from-national-4-h-council/)). This supports DeltaOps' staff-led model, and DeltaOps can cite it with the note that Microsoft supported the survey.

| Streamed game-show quiz | "The Quiz LIVE: MC Gameshow" has a highlights channel with about 419 subscribers ([site](https://thequizlive.com/); [counter](https://realtimesubcount.com/UCisOuVgpYKBFUaE45zUICGg)) | Quiz as performance; small niche |

**What this shows**
- **Blooket and Gimkit.** No Minecraft set with a public play count could be found (agent run; [Blooket](https://www.blooket.com/)). The gap stays open.
- **Quiz channels.** Dedicated Minecraft quiz channels are tiny. The audiences sit with let's-play and roleplay creators (section 6.5). Answering round 2's gap: **there is no large Minecraft quiz-channel audience to borrow from**. The quiz energy lives in *classroom* platforms like Kahoot, not on YouTube.
- **Why quizzes work [inference from the above].** Recall builds an expert identity, the stakes are low, the room shares the moment, and feedback is instant. DeltaOps can borrow all four without any Minecraft content.

### 3.4 Evidence of impact
- **No independent evaluation was found** of AI Foundations, Hour of AI or The First Night (agent run over the [AI Foundations blog](https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/blog/ai-foundations) and the [Hour of AI FAQ](https://edusupport.minecraft.net/hc/en-us/articles/43241143766548-FAQ-Introducing-the-Hour-of-AI)).
- **New peer-reviewed studies:**
  - *Beetrap-MC* (AAAI 2026) is a Minecraft AI-literacy tool about filter bubbles, tested with 9 middle-school students using pre/post measures. It found significant gains, but the sample is tiny ([AAAI](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/41524)).
  - A 2025 DiGRA Japan paper reports positive effects from combining Minecraft with generative AI, but the ages are unclear ([J-STAGE](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/digrajproc/15/0/15_115/_article/-char/en)).
- **General evidence (R2 sources):**
  - Slattery et al. 2025 rate the benefits as "promising" but the evidence quality as limited ([Wiley](https://bera-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/rev3.70035)).
  - A cluster RCT (N=885) found no overall improvement in spatial thinking ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959475224001300)).
  - A negative quasi-experiment exists ([Foro de Educación](https://forodeeducacion.com/ojs/index.php/fde/article/view/769)).
- **Reading:** engagement is well supported, and learning gains are mixed and small-sample. DeltaOps should claim engagement and measure learning itself.

### 7.1 Mojang "Minecraft Usage Guidelines": verbatim quotations (re-fetched 4 November 2026)

Source: [minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines).

**Definitions**
- Brand: "any names, related logos, fonts, textures, and any other distinctive characteristics of any part of our name and games."
- Name includes "any names which are confusingly similar to our name."

**Who the guidelines cover**
- "The allowances we give in these guidelines do not authorize commercial companies, corporate brands, advertising agencies, non-profits, politicians, political action committees, governments to use or exploit Minecraft for promoting products, services, or agendas unrelated to Minecraft."

**Status and discretion**
- "All permissions and consents are given by us at our discretion and may be revoked at any time if we think that it is appropriate to do so, or we don't like what you are doing."
- "We are not able to give advice about whether a specific project does or does not comply with these guidelines. If you are unsure, you should speak to an attorney for help."

**Disclaimer**
- "Prominently include the disclaimer similar to the following: 'NOT AN OFFICIAL MINECRAFT [PRODUCT/SERVICE/EVENT/etc.]. NOT APPROVED BY OR ASSOCIATED WITH MOJANG OR MICROSOFT'".

**Naming and keywords**
- The name may appear only as a secondary name; never "the primary or dominant name or title".
- "Don't use our name as keywords or search tags for products that have no relationship with them".
- Example: "'Minecraft – the ultimate help app' (we're not cool with this)".

**Commercial use**
- "commercial use means any uses of our name, brand, or assets that you use and share with others (regardless of whether you receive payment or provide it for free)."

**Constructed promotions**
- "commercial companies… are not permitted to use Minecraft gameplay to promote or market products, services, events, campaigns, or brands unrelated to Minecraft without prior written approval from Mojang or Microsoft."

**Public display**
- Gameplay must not be "displayed or projected publicly within physical locations", e.g. "Promoting or advertising big-screen Minecraft gameplay inside any commercial venue like restaurants".

**In-person events**
- "limited to no more than approximately 150 participants".
- "You may not accept sponsorships for purposes of profit".
- "Do not use official Minecraft brand or assets on your marketing materials".
- Not OK: "Renting a convention center, charging admission and inviting vendors…".

**Publications**
- "Don't use the Minecraft logo or the word 'Minecraft' in letters that look like, or attempt to look like, the style of our logo".
- Not OK: "a coloring books, poster, sticker book, or chart that just copies our assets".

**Handcrafted goods**
- OK: "A hand-crocheted blanket with the texture pattern of the Creeper".
- Not OK: "A box that features Steve's face in the Minecraft textures but with green eyes".
- Limits: 20 items per design and $5,000 a year.

**Catch-all**
- "If something isn't covered by these guidelines and we haven't otherwise said it's okay, that probably means we don't want you to do it."

### 7.5 Legal principles: UK, EU, US (citations now confirmed; **application to DeltaOps needs a lawyer**)

**UK and EU copyright**
- *Nova Productions v Mazooma Games* [2007] EWCA Civ 219: what inspired the defendants' game was "too general to amount to a substantial part". "If protection for such general ideas… were conferred… copyright would become an instrument of oppression" ([National Archives](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewca/civ/2007/219/data.pdf); [swarb summary](https://swarb.co.uk/nova-productions-ltd-v-mazooma-games-ltd-and-others-ca-14-mar-2007/)).
- *Designers Guild v Russell Williams* [2000] UKHL 58: substantiality is qualitative, and a combination of expressive features can be protected ([BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/uk/cases/UKHL/2000/58.html)).
- *SAS Institute v World Programming*, C-406/10: functionality and programming language are not protected expression ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A62010CJ0406)).

**UK trade marks and passing off**
- Trade Marks Act 1994, s.10(3): reputation marks are protected against use that "takes unfair advantage of, or is detrimental to" them ([legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1994/26/section/10)).
- *L'Oréal v Bellure*, C-487/07: riding on a mark's coat-tails can be unfair advantage even without confusion ([EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=celex%3A62007CJ0487)).
- *Interflora v M&S*, C-323/09: keyword advertising can infringe when users cannot tell the advertiser's origin ([BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/eu/cases/EUECJ/2011/C32309.html)). **Do not bid on "Minecraft".**
- *Reckitt & Colman v Borden* [1990] UKHL 12: passing off needs goodwill, misrepresentation and damage ([BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/uk/cases/UKHL/1990/12.html)).
- *Irvine v Talksport* [2002] EWHC 367 (Ch) ([BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/ew/cases/EWHC/Ch/2002/367.html)) and *Fenty v Arcadia* [2015] EWCA Civ 3 ([BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/ew/cases/EWCA/Civ/2015/3.html)): a false impression of endorsement is actionable.

**Consumer and look-alike rules**
- UK: DMCC Act 2024 Sch. 20 para 15 bans "Promoting a product similar to a product made by a particular manufacturer in such a manner as deliberately to mislead…" ([legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/13/schedule/20); [CMA guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/unfair-commercial-practices-cma207/unfair-commercial-practices)).
- Germany: UWG §4 Nr. 3 covers imitation causing "vermeidbare Täuschung… über die betriebliche Herkunft" (avoidable deception about commercial origin) ([gesetze-im-internet](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__4.html)).

**US**
- *Wal-Mart v Samara*, 529 U.S. 205 (2000): product-design trade dress needs secondary meaning ([govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-529/pdf/USREPORTS-529-205.pdf)).
- *Tetris Holding v Xio*, 863 F. Supp. 2d 394 (D.N.J. 2012): copying the "overall look and feel" infringed even though the rules were free to use ([govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-njd-3_09-cv-06115/pdf/USCOURTS-njd-3_09-cv-06115-0.pdf)).

**Net assessment [inference, for the lawyer to test]**
- A flat-colour, rounded, floating-diorama style with DeltaOps' own characters and no Minecraft elements looks low risk.
- The live risks, in order:
  1. **Words**: names, keywords, "Minecraft-style" copy.
  2. **Endorsement cues.**
  3. **Specific element echoes**: textures, Creeper or Steve likenesses, logo-like font, hotbar, crafting grid, recipes, mob behaviours. This is the Mini World line.
  4. **Automated takedowns** of public imagery or listings.

### 8.1 How the pack works (same in every lab)

- **No devices or typing.** Bodies, hands and the facilitator's screen only. Works with groups of up to about 25, the same cap as Villager Rescue ([FAQ](https://www.minecraftexperience.com/singapore/faq/)).
- **Response modes:**
  - **Corners / A-B-C:** point, hold up fingers, or move to a corner.
  - **Sure-o-meter:** a floor line from "0% Not sure" to "100% Sure".
  - **Thumbs:** up = Trust it, sideways = Check it, down = Don't use it.
- **Slots:** W = warm-up (before Stage 1); S1–S5 = between stages (pick any that fit); P = plenary (after Stage 6).
- **Catchphrase ritual:** the facilitator asks "What did it assume?" and the room shouts "**ASSUMPTION!**" with a hand to the forehead. This is call-and-response energy ([CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/entertainment/article/a-minecraft-movie-has-made-chicken-jockey-a-viral-thing/)) with no IP.
- **Warm-up identity sort ("Which helper are you?").** Three corners:
  - **Speedy Guesser:** "I just go for it!"
  - **Careful Checker:** "I look again before I decide."
  - **Question Asker:** "I ask what you mean first."
  - Facilitator line: "Today our AI helper is a bit of a Speedy Guesser. Your job is to be its Checker and its Asker."
- **Honesty line (new; say it once, in Stage 1).** "Our helper is pretend. We wrote all its answers, including its '% sure', so we can practise spotting mistakes. Real AI helpers also show how sure they are, and they can be wrong too." This addresses the brochure concern that "% sure" implies a live model (`research/context.md`).
- **Key teaching point:** the helper's % shows how sure *it* is, not how right it is.
- **Age adaptation:**
  - **7–9:** corners and thumbs; read the options aloud; children are "the helper's teacher".
  - **10–13:** Sure-o-meter plus "Why there?". Children are **judges** who decide whether the helper's % was fair (section 4).
- **Adult-guidance framing for parents and teachers:** 84% of 9–12-year-olds who use AI want help from adults ([4-H/Microsoft](https://4-h.org/about/blog/young-people-want-guidance-from-adults-on-use-of-ai-tools-says-first-of-its-kind-survey-from-national-4-h-council/)).

### 8.6 3-item exit check (new; all labs; hands or cards, 60 seconds)
1. "The helper says it's 90% sure. Does that mean it's right?" (Correct: no.)
2. "Which card is clearer?" Show one vague and one specific card from today. (Correct: the specific one.)
3. "The helper and the evidence disagree. What do you do?" (Correct: check the evidence or ask a person.)

Record the share of correct hands per item. This gives the "engagement plus a simple learning measure" that section 11 recommends.

- "What did the helper assume?" "What could it have asked?"
- "Was its % sure fair? Too high? Too low?"
- "What can *we* see that the helper can't?"
- Close: "AI helpers can be useful. The clearer we are, and the more we check, the better they work."

## 9. Deliverable B: derivative-risk style checklist

**For a lawyer to review before launch.** Score each item **Green** (fine), **Amber** (review) or **Red** (change before release). Run it on every lab, marketing asset, slide, video and printed item.

**A. Words and names** ([Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines); [partner page](https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/get-started/partners))
- [ ] No "Minecraft", "Mojang", "Creeper", "Steve", "Alex", "Enderman", "Nether", "Redstone", "Minecoins" or "Agent" (as a character) in product or lab names, titles, URLs, handles, hashtags, SEO or ad keywords. *(Red.)*
- [ ] No "-craft" or "Mine-" names (e.g. "AICraft", "MineLab"). *(Red for DeltaOps. "Confusingly similar" names are covered by the guidelines; the Milk Block/"Netcraft" name was litigated ([Guangzhou IP Court](https://www.gipc.gov.cn/front/showArticle?id=2636)); Minecrift was stopped ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2016/08/minecraft-vr-mod-shuts-down-as-microsoft-adds-official-rift-support/)).)*
- [ ] No "Minecraft-style", "like Minecraft" or "for Minecraft fans" in marketing. Do not bid on "Minecraft" as a keyword. *(Red; see [Interflora](https://www.bailii.org/eu/cases/EUECJ/2011/C32309.html).)*
- [ ] Facilitator scripts say "block-building games" generically. *(Green.)*

**B. Visual identity**
- [ ] No pixel-art block textures (16×16 style). Flat colour only. *(Red if present.)*
- [ ] No grass block with the green-fringe-on-brown side; no ore speckles; no TNT, crafting-table, furnace or chest look-alikes. Note that `obj-chest` exists in the brochure: check that it does not echo the Minecraft chest.
- [ ] Characters are not the box-head-on-box-body Steve/Alex silhouette; no square pixel faces. *(Red if present.)*
- [ ] No creatures resembling the Creeper (a registered design mark in Canada ([CIPO](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/1726062))), Enderman, zombie, skeleton, villager, bee or the "chicken jockey".
- [ ] No pixel-form pickaxe, diamond, sword, bed, torch, emerald or golden apple.
- [ ] UI: no 9-slot hotbar, no heart or hunger bars, no 3×3 crafting grid, no grey-bevel inventory, no stone-texture buttons. The rounded app UI is Green.
- [ ] **Font:** no blocky pixel font and nothing resembling the logo lettering. The Allumeria press noted a "Mojangles-looking font" ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/microsoft-withdraws-copyright-claim-against-indie-dev-over-minecraft-allegations/1100-6538103/)), and the publications rule bans logo-style letters. *(Red.)*
- [ ] No square sun or moon, no square clouds.
- [ ] The green-grass, brown-dirt, grey-stone triad is not the dominant palette in **hero images**. The islands do use a grass top, soil band and stone (`research/context.md`). That is generic, but in key art lead with the lab palettes: Roman terracotta, Mars rust, camp turquoise. *(Amber.)*
- [ ] The floating-island diorama framing is DeltaOps' signature. *(Green.)*

**C. Sound and motion**
- [ ] No sound-alikes: block-break crunch, "oof", hiss, C418-like piano, villager "hmm".
- [ ] No punch-to-break animation with crack overlays.

**D. Mechanics, elements and story (the Mini World line)**
- [ ] No copied **specific element designs**: recipes, item combinations, mob behaviours, block types and their rules. Generic mechanics are not protected ([Nova](https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewca/civ/2007/219/data.pdf); [Milk Block](https://www.gipc.gov.cn/front/showArticle?id=2636)), but copied element sets created liability in China ([court report](https://www.sdcourt.gov.cn/lyynfy/390976/390978/9118308/index.html)). *(Red.)*
- [ ] Avoid "survive the first night", "punch a tree" and "craft tools from logs" as framings. "Before sunset" is Green; zombies at dusk are not.
- [ ] Cards, "% sure", meters and visible world change are Green.

**E. Marketing, distribution and takedown readiness**
- [ ] Never place DeltaOps screenshots next to Minecraft screenshots.
- [ ] **"What game is this?" test:** before publishing hero images, video or any store listing, show the image to five children aged 7–11. If more than one says Minecraft, review it. Automated notices fire on resemblance ([Luanti](https://blog.luanti.org/2026/08/27/luanti-dmca-tracer-ai/)).
- [ ] No endorsement cues: no "official" or "approved", no partner badges without a written agreement ([Irvine v Talksport](https://www.bailii.org/ew/cases/EWHC/Ch/2002/367.html)).
- [ ] Merchandise and take-homes carry only DeltaOps characters. *(The partner page bans brand assets on "swag".)*
- [ ] Any truthful mention of Minecraft Education (e.g. a "next steps" sheet) uses plain-text wordmarks only, plus the disclaimer.
- [ ] Never use Minecraft code, assets, texture packs or screenshots in prototypes or pitch decks ([GitHub DMCA](https://github.com/github/dmca/blob/master/2026/08/2026-08-04-mojang.md)).

**F. Process**
- [ ] Name one owner for brand risk. Log decisions.
- [ ] Get one UK IP counsel review of the visual system and names before national launch. Add US counsel if selling there.
- [ ] Keep dated design-provenance files (sketches, palette decisions) as evidence of independent creation.
- [ ] **Takedown plan:** who files a DMCA counter-notice, the backup hosting, and a holding statement. Platforms may restore content only 10–14 business days after a counter-notice, and Luanti's 2023 restoration took 46 days ([Luanti](https://blog.luanti.org/2026/08/27/luanti-dmca-tracer-ai/)).
