# DeltaOps Learning: game research

Research on successful games and what they mean for the DeltaOps world-building AI labs
(the voxel island labs shown in `deltaopslearning-brochure/`). Compiled November 2026 by
parallel research agents using web search. Each report cites its sources; figures marked
[est.] or "unverified" are estimates or unconfirmed.

| 03-narrative-choice.md | Narrative, progression, choice and consequence design, with a sample 6-stage arc |
