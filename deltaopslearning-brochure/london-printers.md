# London printers for the partner guide

Notes for printing `out/delta-learning-partner-guide-print.pdf` (A4, 12 pages, saddle-stitched). Researched 13 October 2026. None of these printers publish prices online, so get quotes from at least two.

## Shortlist

1. **Ex Why Zed** (south-east London): best for a first run of about 50 to 250 copies.
   Designers use it a lot. It runs HP Indigo digital and Heidelberg litho presses. Its house papers come in uncoated, silk and gloss, up to 300gsm. It sends free paper samples, so ask for those first.

2. **Park Communications** (east London, parkcom.co.uk): the premium option.
   Widely seen as London's top-end printer. It offers lamination, special finishes, PUR binding, an HP B2 digital press and a 10-colour Heidelberg litho press. Every job is printed carbon balanced. Use it for a showpiece, or for a bigger run once the content is final. Expect higher prices.

3. **Silver Image London**: the fast option.
   Same-day digital booklets from a London hub. Its premium spec is a 250gsm silk cover with 150gsm silk inner pages. Larger litho runs take 3 to 5 days and cost less per copy.

## What to ask for

- **Format:** A4 portrait, 12 pages, saddle-stitched (stapled).
- **Cover:** 4 pages on 300 to 350gsm (PDF pages 1, 2, 11 and 12), creased, ideally with soft-touch matt lamination on the outside.
- **Inner pages:** 8 pages on 170gsm silk. Silk keeps the bright game colours vivid; uncoated paper makes the mint and sky blue look duller. Going much heavier than 170gsm inside can crack at the fold on a stapled booklet.
- **Proof:** a printed proof before the full run. The PDF is RGB and the printer converts it to CMYK; the colours hold up well, with the mint and sky blue slightly softer.
- **File:** send `out/delta-learning-partner-guide-print.pdf` (3 mm bleed, trim and bleed sizes set). Send `out/delta-learning-partner-guide-print-marks.pdf` only if they ask for crop marks.
