# Block Quiz
Client request · 0:30 · blocky sandbox world (a floating island made of cubes with home-made 16×16 pixel textures, in the spirit of Minecraft but using no Minecraft artwork)

**In one sentence:** A room full of players stands on three coloured answer pads in a pond; an AI helper shouts a confident wrong answer, the players who trusted it drop into the water and lose points, and in the next round the room makes the AI give a reason, tests that reason on a test bench, and everyone gets it right.

**The AI moment:** "What do you need to build a Nether portal?" → AI helper: "Lava!" (90% sure, "WHY: (no reason given)") → most players stand on the yellow Lava pad; the Lava and Diamond pads drop away and the AI's followers splash into the water ("-10 EACH", streak back to 0, "THE AI WAS VERY SURE. AND WRONG.") → the room tells the AI: "Answer, and tell us WHY." → next round, "How do you make obsidian?" The AI says "Water + lava." (85%) with a reason ("Water poured onto still lava turns it into obsidian."); the room presses CHECK IT, a test bench of lava rises out of the pond, water pours on, the lava turns to obsidian ("✓ CHECKED"), and the players stand on the right pad.
