# Build a Space Colony
Career skills · 0:37 · brochure voxel island, Mars version (a blocky red-orange floating island in deep space, against a dark navy-to-cobalt starfield with a small blue Earth and Phobos, lit by a bright warm key light, as in the sister AI Inventor Lab trailer; the same clean white game panels as the other brochure labs)

Test soil first." → "Hydroponic racks under LED lights, in recycled water." (94% sure); a voxel greenhouse builds (clear glass panes in a white frame, two racks of plants under pink and white LED strips), the plants grow and glow, "First harvest: lettuce and tomatoes" and +1 star. A quicker version of the same pattern sets up the site in Level 1 (88% → ice radar → 92% → test drill brings up an ice core, "Ice found ✓ 3 m down", +1 star). Stars and XP are only awarded when a fix is shown working, never at a mistake.
