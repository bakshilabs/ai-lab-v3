# Review: Block Builders trailer (out/blocks.mp4, 40.0 s, 1920x1080)

Game quality: 6.5/10
Visual match: 6.5/10

## Strengths
- One loop with a clear story: paper plan, then screen grid, then island, then the AI guess "Sunny Power Town! Lights on all night" (88%), then lights out at night, then batteries added, then lights on. No rewards at the moment of failure.
- The paper-to-screen-to-voxel build (0–11 s) shows the lab's idea well. The island at 9–15 s is close to the brochure diorama (flat blocks, tiny figure, floating base).
- The HUD is in the brochure's app style: dark AI helper card with GUESS tag and confidence bar, amber Facilitator card, red X and green tick pills. It takes up well under 45% of the frame.
- The tile swap is shown on the board (22–24 s) and the island lighting up (26–30 s) makes a clear payoff.

## Defects (ordered by impact)
1. **Confidence goes down after the fix (27–31 s).** It drops from 88% "Very sure" to 76% "Fairly sure" on the correct answer, which reads as the AI trusting the better plan less. Fix: make the fixed guess 90–93% "Very sure", or lower the first guess to about 70%.
2. **Science conflict with the wind turbines (17–20 s).** The island has 6–8 wind turbines, yet the failure is "Lights out! The sun went down." Children will ask why the turbines didn't keep the lights on. Fix: drop the turbines from the plan and the island, or show them still with a "No wind tonight" note, or change the failure to "Calm, dark night: no sun, no wind."
3. **The fix contradicts itself.** The card says "Swap 3 solar tiles for batteries" (21–25 s) but the summary says "We added 3 batteries." Swapping out solar also means less sunshine to store. The screen board also differs from the paper plan: the 2×2 factory at 6 s has 3 factory tiles at 21 s. Fix: use the card text "Add 3 batteries to store sunshine for night", keep the factory block the same, and put the batteries on empty or house tiles.
4. **Too many names.** The trailer uses "Sunny Power Town", "Eco Town that keeps the lights on", "Habitat Blueprint · Team Foxes" and "Block Builders", plus the roles "Reader / Tapper / Checker". Fix: use one town name throughout and drop "Habitat Blueprint". Either cut the role legend or show it larger with children's hands doing each step.
5. **Text too small.**
   - Tile legend and role legend (1–6 s): about 13 px at 1080p.
   - "Very sure / 88%" label: about 17 px.
   - Summary card body (31–35 s): about 13 px and unreadable.
   Fix: make secondary text at least 20 px, cut the summary card to two lines, or drop it.
6. **Static hold, then a dim ending.** The summary card holds almost unchanged for about 3.5 s (31.5–35 s). The title card then fades to dim around 39.5 s, so the last frame is not a clean title. Fix: cut the summary to about 1.5 s or animate it. Hold the title at full brightness to the final frame, and add the careers line if the series uses one.
7. **Captions without a backing panel, near the bottom edge.** "Build it on paper.", "Copy it to the screen.", "Watch your island rise." and "Change 3 tiles. Test it again." sit at about 91% of frame height. "rise" is cyan on white fog and nearly invisible (8–11 s). Fix: put them on the same rounded pill panel and raise them to about 82%.
8. **Washed-out fog, then murky night.** 7–15 s sits in a white haze that hides the island's base, and the first 2 s of it (7–8 s) is almost blank. 16–25 s is a dark navy scene with a dim island. That is half the trailer away from the brochure's bright daylight look, and it is not a clean starfield either. Fix: use a clear sky-blue backdrop with soft upper-left light for the build and guess. For night, use a deep starfield with a moonlit rim so the island stays readable.
9. **Duplicate guess display (14–16 s).** The "Sunny Power Town · 88%" pill repeats the AI card. Fix: drop the pill.
10. **Children never give the instruction on screen.** The tiles appear with no hands, unlike bloxel. Fix: show a child's hand placing the paper tiles and, at 22–24 s, the battery tiles.

## What would most likely lift each score to 8
- Game quality: make confidence rise after the fix, and remove the wind-turbine contradiction along with the swap/add mismatch.
- Visual match: replace the white fog and murky navy with a bright daylight sky (or a crisp starfield for night), and put the captions on panels with secondary text at 20 px or more.
