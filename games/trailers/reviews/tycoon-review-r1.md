# Review: Island Park Tycoon (tycoon.mp4, 40.0 s, 1920x1080)

Game quality: 6/10
Visual match: 7/10

## Strengths
- Bright, readable voxel park with good scale cues: pond, ducks, flower meadow, kiosks, crowds and tiny blocky visitors. The consequences are physical (visitors flood the meadow path, litter spreads, trees and a hide grow) and are not just shown as numbers.
- Visitors/Wildlife/Tidy HUD is compact (well under 45%) and the bars move with the action (wildlife 70→48, tidy 80→35, then wildlife up to 92).
- Speech bubbles ("My burrow's under that path!", "Crisps… I feel sick.", "Shade at last.") carry the story without sound and suit children.
- The Park Charter recap and the strapline "Manager Bot does what you say. Say what you mean." are strong, and the ending fade matches the reference trailers.

## Defects
1. **Loop structure is broken (0:05–0:30).** Stage 1 fails (card A), then Stage 2 fails with card A again, then the trailer jumps to Stage 5 for a different problem. The clearer cards (Stage 1 C "keep the meadow wild", Stage 2 C "bin by every kiosk") are never chosen, so neither failure gets its fix on screen. The Charter then claims "never cut through the meadow" and "never leave a kiosk without a bin" as lessons learned. Fix: use one fail→fix loop, or at most two. For example, Stage 2 A → litter → choose C → bins appear and Tidy rises. Drop Stage 5, or make it the second loop's fix.
2. **Stage 5 has no wrong guess and the stage numbers are skipped (0:21–0:26).** The trailer goes from Stage 2 to Stage 5. Card C is already selected and the A/B options are never shown. Fix: keep the stage numbers in sequence (1→2) or hide them.
3. **Confidence and badge after the fix (0:26–0:30).** The wrong guesses read 92% and 95%, but the correct plan reads 88%, and its badge still says "GUESS". Fix: make it 96%+ with a "PLAN CHECKED" badge, as in ecotown.
4. **Things vanish without explanation (0:20→0:25).** Six kiosks become two plus a hide. The straight path through the meadow is gone by 0:31. Tidy jumps from 35 to 86 although no bins are ever shown. Fix: show the bins and path removal as on-screen actions, or keep the props.
5. **Grey haze on the choice beats (0:04–0:07, 0:12–0:15) and on the Charter and title (0:31–0:40).** A flat grey veil over the island reads as murky, not as daylight. Ecotown keeps the sky blue behind its cards. Fix: use a light blue sky gradient or a slight blur instead of a grey overlay. Put the title on bright sky.
6. **Captions have no backing panel and sit low (0:01–0:03, 0:10–0:11, 0:26–0:31).** "Meet Manager Bot" is white on a pale sky and fades in. "80 visitors… Did it do what we wanted?" and "Count everyone. Then decide." are about 50 px from the bottom edge, and at 0:30 the "I saw a duckling!" bubble collides with the caption. Fix: use a dark rounded pill and raise the captions to about 120 px from the bottom.
7. **No visible child choice (0:04, 0:12).** Card A is simply highlighted, with no tap or cursor as in the references. Fix: add the hand-cursor tap.
8. **Static hold (0:32–0:35).** The Charter card holds for about 4 s with nothing moving. Fix: trim it to 2 s or animate the rows ticking in.
9. **Small text.** The animal and visitor bubbles ("Lovely log pile.", "No shade", "Too sunny") are about 15–16 px at 1080p, and the dimmed "Tidy" label is hard to read. Fix: use at least 20 px and full opacity.

## To reach 8
- Game quality: rebuild as one or two clean fail→clearer card→fix loops in which the fixed plan shows "PLAN CHECKED" with higher confidence. Never show a lesson in the Charter unless it was fixed on screen.
- Visual match: replace the grey overlays with a bright sky or blur, and give every caption a panel placed higher in the frame.
