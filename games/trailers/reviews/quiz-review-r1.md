# Review: quiz.mp4 ("Sure or Not Sure?"), 37.0 s, 1920x1080

Game quality: 5.5/10
Visual match: 4.5/10

## Strengths
- The opening hook "Can you spot when an AI is guessing?" is clear, and Q1 is a strong beat: a pixel bird, "Duck! 95% sure", a confidence bar, Trust / Check / Don't use it, the room splitting into corners, then "It's a moorhen!" (0:05–0:13).
- The rounded, app-style cards (question card, AI helper card, team scores) match the brochure UI.
- The whole-room mechanic (children walking to coloured corners) reads without sound.
- "Very sure is not the same as right." and "Before I trust an AI's answer, I... CHECK." are good takeaways for children.

## Defects
1. **The required loop is missing (0:05–0:26).** The trailer shows three separate wrong answers (duck, bread, reeds). Nowhere do the children give a clearer instruction, and nowhere does a fix work on screen. Confidence never visibly recovers. This is three failures, not one fail-then-fix loop. Fix: keep Q1 only. After "It's a moorhen!", children pick "Check it", "Give the helper a hint: red beak, white tail", and the helper card re-answers "Moorhen, 90% sure" with a green check. Cut rounds 2 and 3, or cut them down to a 1 s montage.
2. **The whole film is a dark purple stage (0:00–0:37).** It looks like a dark nightclub stage, not a bright daylight island diorama. No grass, trees or water, and no soft upper-left light. The stage is the biggest gap against brochure-refs and the island PNGs. Fix: put the quiz stage outdoors on a floating island (pond, reeds, sky) in daylight, keeping the spotlights as accents.
3. **Reward at the moment of failure (0:12–0:15).** Confetti bursts as the "ASSUMPTION!" wrong answer lands, and covers the bird being revealed. Fix: no confetti on a failure. Use a red shake or a "Hmm" sting, and save confetti for the fixed answer.
4. **Round 3 shows no consequence (0:20–0:24).** The reeds are never shown being cut, and "ASSUMPTION!" pops over the crowd with no backing. Fix: show a reed bed being cleared and a moorhen nest exposed, or drop the round.
5. **Captions have no backing panel.** "Can you spot..." (0:01–0:04), "Very sure is not the same as right." (0:12–0:14), "ASSUMPTION!" (0:12, 0:23), "0% · Not sure / 100% · Sure" (0:15–0:19) and "Before I trust... CHECK." (0:29–0:32) all sit straight on the busy scene, and "ASSUMPTION!" overlaps the characters. Fix: put every caption on a rounded backing panel and keep it above the action.
6. **Half-faded cards overlap the next beat.** The question card ghosts in at 0:06. The bread card and the "Low! Bread is bad for ducks." chip are half-faded over the next shot at 0:17 and 0:19. Fix: hard-cut the cards, or finish each fade before the next beat starts.
7. **A nature fact needs a source and better wording (0:16–0:19).** "Bread is bad for ducks" is too simple. UK guidance (Canal & River Trust, RSPB) says bread has little nutritional value and that leftovers pollute water. Fix: change it to "Bread isn't good for ducks. Try peas or oats." and cite the Canal & River Trust. Also, a misidentified bird is a guess, not an "assumption", so that label confuses the lesson in Q1.
8. **The ending dims (0:36.5–0:37).** The title card fades toward dark (luma falls from about 29 to 17), and the card has no careers line. Fix: hold the title at full brightness to the last frame and add the careers/strapline line.
9. **Small secondary text.** The "QUESTION 1 · TRUST, CHECK..." label, the "Very sure 95%" label on the helper card and the team-score header are roughly 16–18 px at 1080p. Fix: make them at least 22 px.

## What would lift each score to 8
- **Game quality:** replace rounds 2 and 3 with one clear fix beat on Q1 (a clearer hint, then "Moorhen", a confident tick and a reward), so the trailer is guess → fail → clearer instruction → fix, with confetti only at the end.
- **Visual match:** move the stage onto a bright daylight voxel island (grass, pond, reeds, blue sky) and drop the dark purple club look.
