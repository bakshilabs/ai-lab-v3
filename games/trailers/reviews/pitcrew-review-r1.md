# Pit Crew trailer review (out/pitcrew.mp4, 41.0 s, 1920x1080)

Game quality: 6.5/10
Visual match: 6.5/10

## Strengths
- Strong, child-readable concept: "Sprocket learns from the stars you give it" (1-3 s), then a cleanly staged loophole: STARS counter climbing 7→43 while LAPS stays 0 (15-19 s).
- The first failure is easy to read: the kart ploughs a brown scar through the meadow and the Meadow meter falls from 100% to 55% (7-11 s).
- The fix pays off on screen: the kart stays on the dirt road through the gates, Meadow goes back up to 99% and Laps reaches 1 (28-33 s).
- Gameplay shots match the brochure: a floating voxel island, flat-colour flower blocks, a pond with ducks, and rounded white app cards.

## Defects
1. **Confidence drops after the fix (25-27 s).** The guesses read 92% (5 s) and 97% (13 s), then the corrected plan reads "Fairly sure 74%". Fix: show 95% or more on the post-fix Sprocket card.
2. **Numbers don't match (17-19 s).** The facilitator card says "47 stars" while the HUD reads 25, 34, then 43, and never reaches 47. Fix: let the counter land on 47 before the card appears, or change the copy to match.
3. **A stage is skipped (4 s, 12 s, 25 s).** The labels read STAGE 1 → STAGE 2 → STAGE 4. The Stage 4 recipe card also shows only option "C", with no A or B. Fix: rename it to STAGE 3 and show the three choices, or drop the letter.
4. **Ends on a fade to dark (40.3-41.0 s).** Luma falls from 98 to 31. The title card is also a dim grey vignette that holds static for about 4 s (37-41 s). Fix: end on a bright, sky-lit title card that holds about 2 s, with no fade.
5. **Murky grey overlay screens (4-6, 11-14, 24-27, 37-41 s; about 13 of 41 s).** The island is shown desaturated under a grey fog, not in bright daylight. Fix: lighten the backdrop to a pale blue sky, or use a light frosted panel, and keep the scene saturated.
6. **Leftover and half-faded panels (7 s, 24 s, 26 s, 29 s).** A ghost Meadow HUD sits top right at 7 s. The faded "trial and error" caption overlaps the penalty-dial card at 24 s. A half-faded Sprocket card is still on screen at 26 s, and the "Same kart. Better recipe." caption sits under the orange gate at 29 s. Fix: make the cuts hard, or finish each fade before the next card appears.
7. **Text and layout (throughout).** Choice-card, facilitator and licence body text is about 14-16 px at 1080p. The facilitator card sits about 50 px from the bottom edge at 17 s. "TRAINING GARAGE" is clipped at the left edge at 20-23 s. Captions have no backing panel (1-3, 9-11, 21-23, 29-32 s). Fix: set secondary text to at least 18 px, add a 48 px safe margin, and put a soft pill behind captions.
8. **Static choice holds (4-6 s, 12-14 s).** Each holds about 3 s with only a card fade-in. Fix: animate a cursor tapping the chosen card.

## What would most likely lift each score to 8
- **Game quality:** fix the confidence (95% or more after the fix), the 47-star mismatch and the skipped stage number, and end cleanly on a bright title card.
- **Visual match:** replace the grey fogged overlay and title backdrops with a bright sky-lit diorama, and clear the ghost panels and the text that is too small or clipped.
