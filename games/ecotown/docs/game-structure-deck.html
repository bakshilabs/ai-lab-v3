At a glance

A city builder with an AI Lab inside it

  • A browser game (three.js voxels) on a floating island.
  • Players build a whole sustainable town: land, transport, homes, food, power and water, a town heart.
  • Florence, a wooden-robot AI planner, helps. She is often confidently wrong.
  • Children learn to check her with evidence before trusting her plans.
  • Made for a full-day kids' club, schools and museums.
Two versions

Ages 8–12 and ages 13–17, one engine.

Honest simulation

Every lens, bubble and light uses one true simulation. Only Florence is ever wrong.

The lesson

“Sure is not the same as right.”

The core loop

Build, get advice, step into the Lab, come back

1

Build

Free building on the island. Nothing blocks the player. Residents move in and live while they build.

2

Florence advises

Short tips when something changes (at most one every 25 s). Every ~2.5 min she suggests a plan, often wrong.

3

AI Lab mission

From 🧪 or “Show me in the AI Lab”. A short lesson on Florence's practice island, never the player's town.

4

Bring back a lens

Each mission earns a lens. The player uses it to check their own town and Florence's next ideas.

↺ Back to building, with a new lens and a better eye for Florence's mistakes

The AI Lab · six missions

Each mission is one confident mistake

Florence is a small language model plus a literal planner, so the mistakes come from the player's own words. A clearer instruction can honestly lower her “% sure”.

Inside one mission

From a vague instruction to a checked plan

Opening episode

Brief & plan

2

Check

A lens, street view or inside view reveals the flaw.

3

Fix

Clearer instruction, then build it and watch the construction film.

★

Up to 3 stars; the 3rd for spotting the hidden guess.

🎬

Closing episode

The eco idea and the AI habit.

And takes away

The mission's lens in the top-right strip, to check their own town from then on.

Florence outside the Lab

Three ways Florence talks to the player

Tips

Light advice

One line from the honest simulation, only when a rule turns true. “Show me in the AI Lab” when it matches a mission. “Well done” when a problem clears.

Suggestions

Catch the AI

A pale ghost plan with “I'm 89% sure!”. Most are wrong. Check it with a lens, then say right or wrong. A catch earns ★1; using a flawed plan unchecked gets an honest “oops”.

Classroom

Ask Florence

Inside the AI Lab building: typed or suggested questions, answered from a fixed, child-safe set, with the matching build film on the big screen.

Reactions

The nearest family comments on each build; animals arrive or leave; night events every third day (flood, blackout).

Character

A 3D wooden robot who walks the island, points, cheers and pulls an “oops” face when caught. Recorded voices for both age bands.

Rewards and long-term goals

What keeps a child playing all day

Stars

★ and the star shop

Stars from missions and catches buy decorations: hats, flag, fountain, bee statue, fireworks.

Badges

15 badges

Dry feet, Car-free town, Bee friend, Sharp eyes, Check first, Gold town and more.

Passport

Club passport

139 points from missions, town, medals, challenges and catches. Six ranks, Helper to Chief Planner.

Certificate

Print to take home

One A4 page. No name is stored; there is a line to write it on.

6

Ranks: Helper 0 · Builder 8 · Planner 20 · Town Planner 35 · Master Planner 65 · Chief Planner 100. The first town and missions alone stop at Town Planner; Chief Planner needs most challenges at silver or better.

Replay

Beyond the first town

3 islands

Home, Delta, Hills

Delta: flood meadow and marsh, little dry land. Hills: old wood, terraces and a long beach. Missions run on the home island.

13 challenges

Challenge towns

One rule each, opened after the six missions: Florence's Town, Tiny Town, Light Footprint, The Still Island, Wild Island, Room for Everyone, Eight-Minute Town, plus three on each new island.

Sharing

Show your town

A town travels as a code or QR, typed or scanned in the same room. Visit read-only, a club wall of up to 60 towns, and a postcard picture.

Free island towns

Build freely on Delta or Hills (“island of the day” for a club leader).

Safety rules for sharing

No accounts, no chat, no free text between devices, no personal data, no server.
