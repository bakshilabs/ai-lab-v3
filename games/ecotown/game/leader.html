Eco Town: club leader's one-page plan

A full day for ages 8 to 12 or 13 to 17. Children build a town with Florence, an AI planner who is sometimes confidently wrong, and learn to catch her mistakes with evidence. You do not need to know the game: read this page once.

The day

WhenWhat
0:00Watch Florence's intro on one screen. Everyone names a town and marks three tiles.
0:15Build. Do AI Lab missions 1 to 3 as they open (the AI Lab button shows a number).
1:30Break.
1:45Missions 4 to 6. Finish the eight town goals (tap the flower).
3:00Lunch.
3:45Open Challenge towns together. Read the rules aloud; guess the hardest.
4:00Challenge towns in pairs. Start with "Florence's Town".
5:15Break.
5:30Second round: a higher medal, or a challenge not tried yet.
6:15Town meeting: the five questions. Print certificates from the Club passport.

Before the children arrive

  • Open the game on every device and check the island appears. Sound on, or headphones.
  • Choose "Ages 8–12" or "Ages 13–17". For a group that should stay together, choose "In order (class)".
  • A second day, or shared devices? Add ?challenges=1 to the game's address to open the challenge towns straight away.
  • Towns save themselves on each device. The same child should use the same device after a break.

Five questions to ask

  1. "Florence said she was 90% sure. Was she right? How did you find out?"
  2. "Which lens showed you something you could not see from above?"
  3. "What did Florence guess because you did not tell her?"
  4. "Who was not asked when the school was planned? What changed when you asked them?"
  5. "Before you trust an AI's plan, what three things will you do?"

If a child is stuck

  • Do not know what to do: read the "Next" line at the top left, or tap the flower for the goals.
  • Tabs are locked: the families need homes, then food. The "Next" line says how many.
  • A challenge will not pass: tap the flower. The last lines are the challenge's own rule.
  • Finished early: Menu, Club passport. It names the next thing worth points.
  • Something looks broken: reload the page. The town is saved.
What they should leave knowing. An AI follows words exactly, fills gaps with guesses, and can sound sure while being wrong. Say clearly what you want, ask what it guessed, and check its plan against evidence.

Safety and data

No accounts, no chat, no adverts, no purchases. Nothing a child types leaves the device. Certificates have a blank line for the name.
